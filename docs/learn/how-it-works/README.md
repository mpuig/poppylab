# How it works

What a customer, their agent and a company each do when something gets done
through Personal Agent Protocol, shown one screen at a time. Each page follows
one everyday process from the first request to the result, on the agent's side
and on the company's own website.

The pages are written for readers who want to understand the process before
the protocol. Each step says in plain words what is happening and who decides.
What travelled between the agent and the company is folded under each step
for those who want it.

| Process                                   | What it shows                                                                                                   |
| ----------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| [Changing an order](changing-an-order.md) | A customer asks her agent to change a restaurant order. The company sets the terms and she approves them first. |

Every screenshot comes from the live lab at [poppylab.dev](https://poppylab.dev)
and its four fictional companies, so each page is also a record of that process
working end to end on the day it was captured. Nothing in them is real: no
account, money, food or flight.
