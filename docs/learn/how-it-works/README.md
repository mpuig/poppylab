# How it works

What a customer, their agent and a company each do when something gets done
through Personal Agent Protocol, shown one screen at a time. Each page follows
one everyday process from the first request to the result, on the agent's side
and on the company's own website.

The pages are written for readers who want to understand the process before
the protocol. Each step says in plain words what is happening and who decides.
What travelled between the agent and the company is folded under each step
for those who want it.

| Process                                                                   | What it shows                                                                                                                                  |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| [Connecting to a company](connecting-to-a-company.md)                     | What a company publishes so that any agent can work with it, and what an agent can do before the customer has signed in.                       |
| [Signing in and giving permission](signing-in-and-giving-permission.md)   | The customer connects her account on the company's own page, to read first and to propose changes later, and takes the access back.            |
| [Changing an order](changing-an-order.md)                                 | A customer asks her agent to change a restaurant order. The company sets the terms and she approves them first.                                |
| [Moving money](moving-money.md)                                           | A transfer and a payment at a bank, each approved by the customer, and what she sees when the bank refuses.                                    |
| [Changing a trip](changing-a-trip.md)                                     | A traveller accepts an upgrade and adds a bag. The airline sets each price and each change is approved on its own.                             |
| [Letting the agent approve for you](letting-the-agent-approve-for-you.md) | A standing instruction with a limit lets the agent approve small changes, and the company knows the agent approved.                            |
| [Getting help from the company](getting-help-from-the-company.md)         | The customer's agent talks to the company's own agent about an order, and asks for a person.                                                   |
| [Talking to the company yourself](talking-to-the-company-yourself.md)     | The company asks to talk to the customer directly. Her agent steps aside, and a refund still needs her approval.                               |
| [When the company can't do it](when-the-company-cant-do-it.md)            | The customer asks for something the company does not offer to agents, and the agent tells the company without any personal data.               |
| [When something goes wrong](when-something-goes-wrong.md)                 | A declined proposal, an approval that expires, terms that change and a request the company refuses. Nothing is done twice or without approval. |

Every screenshot comes from the live lab at [poppylab.dev](https://poppylab.dev)
and its four fictional companies, so each page is also a record of that process
working end to end on the day it was captured. Nothing in them is real: no
account, money, food or flight.
